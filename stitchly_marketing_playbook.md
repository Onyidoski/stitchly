## 1. Is Stitchly a Good, Monetisable Product?

**Yes — with the right focus and market.**

### What Works in Your Favour
- **Real, painful problem** — designers run businesses on paper, notebooks & WhatsApp
- **Underserved niche** — almost no tool built specifically for fashion designers in Africa
- **Low competition locally** — global tools (HoneyBook, Studio Designer) are USD-priced and overcomplicated
- **Recurring need = recurring revenue** — designers never stop getting clients
- **Willingness to pay** — a designer doing 20 orders/month at ₦50,000 each will happily pay ₦4,500/month

### Honest Challenges
- Sales is harder than building — you'll need to go to designers, they won't search for you
- Small ARPU means you need volume (~200 users for ₦1M/month)
- Churn risk if the app is hard to use on mobile
- Some designers prefer one-time payments over subscriptions

### Verdict

| Criteria | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Problem clarity | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| Market size | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| Willingness to pay | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| Competition (low = good) | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| Distribution difficulty | ⭐⭐ (hardest part) |

## 2. Pricing Strategy

### Recommended Model: Single Flat Plan + Free Trial

```
Sign Up
   ↓
30-day FREE trial (full access to everything)
   ↓
Trial ends
   ↓
Pay to continue  →  ₦4,500/month
                 →  ₦45,000/year (saves ₦9,000 — 2 months free)

        OR

Don't pay  →  Account goes read-only
               (can view data, cannot add/edit)
```

### Why This Model?
- Easy to explain: "₦4,500/month, everything included"
- No plan paralysis — one decision to make
- 30-day full trial removes all risk for the designer
- By day 30, their data is in the app — best reason to pay
- Annual plan = upfront cash for you + commitment from them
- Read-only (not hard lock) on expiry = friendly + keeps them coming back

### Why NOT Freemium (Yet)
- Freemium works at scale, not at launch
- Free users rarely convert and create support burden
- Kills perceived value in this market

### Why NOT Multiple Tiers (Yet)
- You don't yet know what features designers truly value
- Wait until 50+ users, then add tiers based on real data

### Pricing Psychology
- ₦4,500/month = 0.6% of a ₦750,000/month designer's revenue. It's a no-brainer.
- Don't go below ₦3,000 — cheap pricing signals low quality
- Always show annual price as "save 2 months"

## 3. Marketing Strategy

### Phase 1: Get Your First 20 Users (Free, Right Now)

**Instagram & WhatsApp DMs (Most Effective)**
- Search hashtags: `#LagosTailor` `#NigerianFashionDesigner` `#AbujaFashion` `#MadeinNigeria`
- Send personal (not broadcast) messages:
  > "Hi [Name], I love your work! I built a free tool for fashion designers to manage clients, measurements & invoices in one place. Would you be open to trying it free for 30 days?"

**Your Personal Network**
- Start with any designer you know personally
- Ask them to refer one other designer in exchange for extended free trial

**Facebook Groups to Join**
- Nigerian Fashion Designers Network
- Lagos Fashion Community
- African Tailors & Designers
- Post value first, mention Stitchly naturally

---

### Phase 2: Build an Audience (Time, Not Money)

**TikTok / Instagram Reels — Most Powerful Channel**

Content ideas:
- "How I used to track my 30 clients as a fashion designer 😭" → show chaos → show Stitchly
- "Day in the life of a fashion designer using Stitchly"
- "Stop losing client measurements — here's a better way"
- "How to send professional invoices as a tailor in Nigeria"
- Screen recordings of the app in action

**Twitter/X**
- Build in public: share your journey building Stitchly
- Tweet tips: "5 common mistakes fashion designers make with client payments"

**YouTube Shorts**
- Tutorial: "How to manage your fashion design clients without paper"
- These also rank on Google — free SEO traffic

---

### Phase 3: Partnerships (High Leverage)

**Fashion Schools**
- Offer students free access → they graduate and keep using it
- Ask to give a 15-min demo to a class

**LSETF / Fashion Associations**
- These groups have WhatsApp communities of designers
- One partnership = hundreds of potential users

**Fabric Markets (Balogun, Idumota, Wuse Market)**
- Leave flyer with QR code
- Partner with fabric vendors: they refer designers, you offer discounts

**Micro-Influencer Fashion Designers (10K–50K followers)**
- Give lifetime Pro access in exchange for an honest story/reel review
- Nano-influencers convert better than celebrities for niche tools
